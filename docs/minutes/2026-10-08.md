## Achievements: loops closed from Progress Report 1

Each of these was raised as a problem in a Progress Report 1 meeting and had been
resolved by this session. The evidence is on `main`.

1. **"HinGE's released splits are not mutually exclusive."** The splits were re-derived
   from 1,938 unique sources, grouped into 1,937 near-duplicate clusters. The leakage gate
   reports **0 exact and 0 near-duplicate overlaps** at Jaccard ≥ 0.7
   (`reports/data_statistics.json`, `leakage_gate`). The resulting 3,738 / 468 / 468 splits
   are committed with SHA-256 hashes in `data/processed/manifests/hinge_split_manifest.json`.
2. **"An earlier draft named a model configuration that does not exist."** The technology
   survey now resolves every model identifier against its official Hugging Face model card
   and gives the command to repeat the check (`docs/workbook1/section_1_4.md`, check dated
   8 Oct).
3. **"Reference count 4,803 contradicted by secondary sources."** The pipeline measured
   **4,799** references directly off the file. Both figures are reported in
   `docs/datasheet.md`, and the reconciliation is still open rather than hidden.
